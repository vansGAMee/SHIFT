SHIFT audio credits — 1.1.0

ORIGINAL AUTHOR TRACKS
The author of SHIFT supplied these original personal tracks and requested their
inclusion in the game and its public repository:
  author/shift1.mp3 — quiet, one-shot playback beginning in room four.
  author/shift.mp3 — one-shot playback during the finale and end credits.
Both tracks retain their original audio data. Neither track loops.
Original music is credited to SHIFT's author in both language versions.

ADDITIONAL SOUND
score.ogg: deterministic electronic background composition generated by
tools/generate_score.py. Additional memory, telephone, wall-break, wind,
room-tone, footsteps and interaction effects were synthesized for SHIFT.
Development and visual implementation used AI tools under the author's direction.

LEGACY DEMO FILE
The unrelated legacy assets/audio/music.mp3 is excluded from public source,
exported games and release archives. It is not either author track above.

Streamer mode mutes all music, including author tracks, while retaining effects.
